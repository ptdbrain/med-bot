# MedTutor Product Context

## Register

Product UI.

## Users

Medical students and learners who repeatedly ask questions across trusted educational documents and need to inspect evidence before accepting an answer.

## Product Purpose

Provide a safe medical-learning RAG workspace for document ingestion, grounded question answering, source verification, and retrieval evaluation.

## Strategic Principles

- Evidence is part of the answer, not a secondary debug view.
- Medical safety takes priority over conversational fluency.
- RAG quality is measured through evaluation, not demo impressions.
- Only implemented capabilities appear in the interface.
- The application is not for diagnosis, prescriptions, or personalized treatment.

## Tone

Calm, precise, academic, and direct. Vietnamese is the default interface language.

## Anti-References

- Marketing SaaS dashboards.
- Decorative healthcare illustrations.
- Hospital-white interfaces with saturated teal everywhere.
- AI chat products that hide retrieval evidence.
